Your Inbox books people the way a salon is booked: with Mei, or with whoever is free. Customers' assistants read who gives what, find a time with them, and book it; you answer in the app, or let your rules confirm the ones you are sure of.

Settings → **Services** → **Staff**, or **A salon or barber** in the setup wizard.

## Staff

- **Who they are.** The name you know them by stays yours. Customers see a first name, or the name you choose (where names are written family name first, choose it), and a photo if you add one. Nothing else: no surname, no hours, no phone.
- **What each gives.** One press per service. A senior stylist's own price, or a longer cut, is set beside the service; everyone else has the service's own.
- **Their hours.** Their own week, or the salon's.
- **Their calendar.** Connect it in Settings → Integrations, then choose it under their name. Their busy times take only their times off, and only their bookings go into it. A calendar tied to nobody stays the salon's: its busy times close everyone.

A booking for **anyone** goes to the first person free, in your order. A booking asked for someone stays with them; if they are not free then, a person answers it, as any request for a taken time.

The inbox home shows the day **by staff**: a column each, a request dashed until it is confirmed.

## A service's terms

Settings → Services → **Terms**, beside each service.

- **Add-ons**: "Blow-dry, 15 min, $20". Each makes the booking longer and, if priced, dearer.
- **Deposit**: none, a share (20 %, 50 %) or an amount. It is asked once the booking is confirmed, on your own Stripe or Mollie, in your currency's own units (₩10,500, ¥3,000, KSh 1,500). Not paid in time, the link closes and you decide: nothing is cancelled for you. The rest is paid at the visit.
- **Cancelling**: start from *Free until 24 h before*, *Free until 48 h, then 50 %* or *Deposit kept under 24 h*. Some places limit cancellation fees and deposits: check yours. This is not legal advice.
- **Comes after**: a colour after a patch test within six months, say.

Customers read who it is with, the add-ons, the deposit and the policy, dated in your time zone, before they confirm. When they cancel and it costs something, they are told the fee and what comes back first, and it is cancelled only once they say yes. The fee is yours to take, from the deposit or with your own link: your Inbox never charges anyone.

## What must come first

Checked only against your own bookings with that client. When the inbox knows the client for sure (they come back through their own booking, or their assistant's key is theirs), a client who has not had the patch test is asked to book it first. When it cannot know who they are, the booking waits for you with a note of what to check, and no rule confirms it. Nobody is ever shown anyone's records.

## New or returning

The salon rules confirm a returning client (one with a visit here and no no-shows) at once when their stylist is free in their hours, and send a new client's first visit to you. New or returning comes only from your own bookings. On a hosted inbox you confirm every booking yourself until you switch the rules on.

## Your booking system

A salon that runs its days in a booking system can start from it and keep it the authority: each booking is written there first and confirmed here only once it took it. See [Phorest](/docs/connectors/phorest/) and [Mindbody](/docs/connectors/mindbody/). Salons on Fresha, Booksy, Timely or Vagaro send agents to their own booking page instead (Settings → General); those systems publish nothing a connection can be built from.
