A request for a price from a customer's assistant can say what the job is, how urgent, whether you should see it first, where it is, when suits, and bring the photos. You decide where you go, what coming out costs, and which requests go into the tool you run your jobs in.

Settings → **Area** and **Work**.

## Where you work

- **A distance**, measured from your premises (as Settings → General has them on the map) or from where your phone is when you set it: **Use where I am**. Neither is ever shown to anyone. Agents are told the distance, and a site is told back to you as a band: *inside your area · within 10 km*, never a distance to your door.
- **Postal codes**, or their start: `04` covers every code from 04000 to 04999; in the UK, `M1` covers M1 1AE and not M12. Each country's codes are checked in their own shape.
- **Anywhere.**

A request from outside is yours to decide, or declined, as you choose. When a site cannot be checked — no point for a distance, a postal code that is not one — the request comes to you: nothing is ever said to be inside when it is not known.

## How you take work

- **Call-out fee**, and whether accepting your quote takes it off.
- **Outside opening hours**, what a visit adds: an amount, or a share. Opening hours are in Settings → Availability.
- **Emergencies online**: not taken, only while you are open, or at any hour — always only inside your area. An emergency comes first in your inbox. One you do not take is answered with what to do instead: ask within 24 hours, or call you.
- **Free estimates**, said in your public profile.
- **Site visits**: choose the service a visit is booked as, and a customer's assistant books one beside the request it is for. You confirm it.

## Photos and plans

A request can carry up to six files: photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP) and plans (PDF), up to 10 MB each. An iPhone's HEIC photo is refused with *send it as a JPEG*; most phones can share a photo as JPEG.

- **Where a photo was taken is taken out.** Every photo is cleaned on arrival: its location, the time, the phone, any thumbnail of what was cropped. Only which way up it is stays.
- **Yours alone.** They are kept in your inbox, never published, never sent to a network. You open them from the request, through links that work for ten minutes.
- **Gone with the customer.** Erasing a customer erases their files.
- A hosted inbox keeps up to 500 MB of files.

## Quotes in stages

On a request, **Quote in stages…**: an estimate or a fixed price, the total, and up to four stages that add up to it — a deposit now, a stage by a date, the balance on the day. Whether your prices include tax. The customer's assistant shows your person the whole of it before they say yes: *This is an estimate. It is paid in stages: Deposit 1,000.00 now; On completion 3,800.00 on the day. Prices include tax.*

An accepted quote in stages asks for no payment of the whole: you ask for each stage when it is due.

## Your job tool

Settings → Integrations → **Job tool**. See [Job tools](/docs/job-tools/) for what each one does.

Which requests go in is yours to choose, in Settings → Work:

- **Only ones I take on** (the default): a request goes in when you quote it, or when you press **Send to …** on it.
- **Every request**, as it arrives.
- **Never.**

A test request never goes in.
