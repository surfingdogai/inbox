Surfing Dog Inbox is the phone app for your business's Surfing Dog Inbox. New booking requests, orders, quote requests and messages reach your phone as notifications, and you answer them from there. Your inbox stays on your own website.

What it does
• Notifications for booking and stay requests, orders, quote requests and messages. Choose which kinds, and set quiet hours that hold them until morning.
• Notifications carry no customer name unless you turn on Show details.
• Accept, propose another time or decline. Another time is picked from the free times your inbox offers.
• Reply to a customer from the request.
• Today: the day's bookings at a glance.
• When your inbox's spam filter holds something, see why, then let it in or mark it as spam.
• Every inbox you look after in one app, for people who look after more than one business.
• Works offline: what you do waits on the phone and is sent when you're back.
• Opens with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. An Accept from a notification opens the request and asks for your fingerprint or face first; nothing is done until you press it in the app.

Connect it in one scan
You need a Surfing Dog Inbox, on your own server or hosted by Surfing Dog, and a screen lock on the phone.
1. On a computer, open your inbox → Settings → Phone → Add a phone. A code appears.
2. In the app, tap Scan the code. It asks to connect to your inbox and shows two digits.
3. Check the same two digits on the computer and press Confirm. Nothing to type.
No camera? The app can ask your inbox to email you a sign-in link.
No inbox yet? Tap Try the demo on the first screen to look round a made-up bike shop. Nothing you press there is sent.

Privacy
• Notifications are encrypted by your inbox for your phone alone. They travel through Surfing Dog's push relay and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, which can't read them.
• The camera only reads the code from your inbox. The picture never leaves the phone.
• What the app keeps on the phone is encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore, and never backed up.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reports.
What the app sends, and to whom: surfingdog.ai/privacy

Good to know
• Notifications need Google Play services. On some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Huawei), allow background activity for Surfing Dog Inbox, or notifications can wait until you open it.
• Android 7.0 or later.
• Open source, MIT licensed: github.com/surfingdogai/inbox
