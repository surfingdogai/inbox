No account, code, email or payment is needed.
1. Open the app. On the first screen, Connect your inbox, tap Try the demo.
2. The app opens Oficina Maré, a made-up bike shop we run at demo.surfingdog.ai, with example bookings, orders, quote requests and messages. It is set up again every night.
3. Look round Inbox, any request, Today and Settings. The demo is read-only: Confirm, Reply and the switches say so and send nothing.
4. To leave: Leave, at the top of the inbox.
