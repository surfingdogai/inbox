RALCAI

Guardrails

  • At most two stacked backdrop-filter layers. List rows are translucent without blur. Only nav, panels and sheets are true glass, with an @supports fallback.
  • Colour never fills a surface and never colours body text. Item types are dots, rules and tinted pills. States are palm, sun and #e5484d, the last for destructive actions only.
  • WCAG AA on glass in both themes: every tinted pill uses a text-safe ink, and the primary button is ink on white by day, white on ink by night.
  • Motion only in response to user action. Reduced motion is respected. The light fields are static.
  • The greyscale test in the top bar removes all hue. If a screen stops reading, the colour was doing a job it should not have.
